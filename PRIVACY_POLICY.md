# BHAIWAY PRIVACY POLICY

**Effective Date:** 04/08/2026  
**Last Updated:** 04/08/2026  

This Privacy Policy (“Policy”) describes how BhaiWay Pvt Ltd (“BhaiWay”, “Company”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) collects, uses, processes, stores, shares, protects, and manages personal information when users access or use the BhaiWay mobile application, website, APIs, customer support services, and related products (collectively referred to as the “Platform”).

By accessing, registering, or using BhaiWay, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. ABOUT BHAIWAY

BhaiWay is a technology-enabled ride-sharing and community mobility platform that facilitates connections between verified users for:
- Outstation rides
- Intercity rides
- Daily office commute rides
- Corporate commute programs
- Women-only rides
- Assured rides
- Regular rides

BhaiWay is not a transportation company, taxi operator, cab service provider, fleet owner, or employer of drivers. BhaiWay acts solely as a technology platform that enables users to connect and coordinate rides.

---

## 2. DEFINITIONS

For the purpose of this Policy:
- **Account** means a registered BhaiWay profile.
- **Driver** means a verified user who publishes or offers a ride.
- **Rider** means a verified user who books or joins a ride.
- **Personal Data** means any information that identifies or relates to an identifiable individual.
- **Sensitive Personal Data** includes Aadhaar details, government identification documents, location data, and verification information.
- **Corporate User** means a user verified through corporate email and corporate identity verification.
- **Trust Score** means BhaiWay’s internal reputation and verification metric.

## 3. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

### 3.1 Registration Information
During registration, we may collect:
- Full Name
- Mobile Number
- Email Address
- Gender
- Profile Photograph
- Date of Birth

### 3.2 Verification Information
To maintain safety and trust, BhaiWay may collect:

- Aadhaar Verification Status

- Corporate ID Card
- Corporate Email Verification

### 3.3 Vehicle Information
For Drivers:
- Vehicle Make
- Vehicle Model
- Registration Number
- Vehicle Type
- Vehicle Color
- Manufacturing Year
- EV Status (if applicable)

### 3.4 Ride Information
We collect:
- Pickup Locations
- Drop Locations
- Travel Routes
- Ride Preferences
- Ride History
- Passenger Count
- Ride Schedules
- Booking Information
- Cancellation History

### 3.5 Live Location Information
When enabled by the user, BhaiWay may collect:
- Real-time GPS Location
- Active Ride Tracking
- Route Navigation Data
- Ride ETA Information

Location data may be collected before, during, and after rides where required for ride completion, fraud prevention, safety monitoring, and customer support.

### 3.6 Emergency Information
Users may voluntarily provide:
- Emergency Contact Details

- SOS Alerts
- Safety Reports
- Incident Reports

### 3.7 Wallet & Payment Information
We may collect:
- Wallet Balance
- Transaction Records
- Payment History

### 3.8 Customer Support Information
When users contact support, we may collect:
- Complaint Details

- Ticket Information
- Uploaded Documents
- Photographs
- Screenshots
- Investigation Notes

### 3.9 Device Information
We may automatically collect:
- Device ID

- Browser Information
- Login Activity
- Security Logs

## 4. WHY WE COLLECT YOUR DATA

We process information for the following purposes:
- **Account Creation:** To create and manage user accounts.
- **Identity Verification:** To verify identity and maintain platform safety.
- **Ride Matching:** To connect riders and drivers.
- **Corporate Verification:** To verify eligibility for corporate commute services.
- **Trust Score Management:** To calculate and maintain trust ratings.
- **Wallet Management:** To process wallet transactions and settlements.
- **Ride Execution:** To facilitate ride bookings, tracking, and completion.
- **Fraud Prevention:** To detect abuse, fake accounts, duplicate registrations, and suspicious activity.
- **Safety Protection:** To investigate incidents and support SOS functions.
- **Customer Support:** To resolve disputes and support requests.
- **Legal Compliance:** To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and lawful requests.

## 8. TRUST SCORE SYSTEM

BhaiWay maintains a trust score system that may consider:

- Ride completion history
- Ratings and reviews
- Safety incidents
- Cancellation history
- No-show incidents

Trust scores may affect visibility, eligibility, and access to certain services.

## 9. HOW WE SHARE INFORMATION

We may share information with:
- **Other Users:** Limited information necessary for ride coordination.

- **Verification Providers:** For identity verification.
- **Customer Support Teams:** For issue resolution.
- **Law Enforcement Agencies:** Where legally required.
- **Courts and Regulatory Authorities:** Where required by law.

## 10. DATA RETENTION

Unless a longer retention period is required by law:
- **Account Records:** During account life cycle.
- **Ride Records:** Up to 5 years.
- **Wallet Records:** Up to 8 years.
- **Support Tickets:** Up to 5 years.
- **Investigation Records:** Up to 7 years.

Certain information may be retained after account deletion for legal, safety, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes.

## 11. ACCOUNT DELETION

Users may request account deletion through the Platform. Deletion requests may be denied or delayed if:
- There are active disputes.
- Fraud investigations are pending.
- Legal obligations require retention.

Deleted accounts may lose access permanently to wallet balances, trust scores, ride history, and rewards.
